# Digital Scrapbook

A lightweight desktop scrapbook for capturing and composing ideas instantly.

## ✨ Overview
Digital Scrapbook is a freeform visual canvas that lets you collect inspiration directly from your screen and turn it into a personalized journal.

Instead of saving images manually, you can quickly capture content and place it onto your canvas, where everything stays flexible and editable.

## 🧩 Features
- 📸 Instant capture from screen
- 🧷 Freeform canvas layout
- 🔄 Drag, scale, rotate elements
- 🎚️ Opacity adjustment
- 🗂️ Layer management
- 📓 Scrapbook-style visual experience

## 🎨 Concept
Designed to mimic the feeling of a real handmade journal, this tool focuses on creativity, freedom, and visual storytelling — turning everyday inspiration into a living scrapbook.
